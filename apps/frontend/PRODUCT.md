# Product

Scope: users, purpose and design principles of the product, for UI work.

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

- Primary: enterprise knowledge workers who research, draft, analyze documents and run approved workflows. They need to see and approve the tool calls that change data, without learning the admin settings.
- Secondary: CTOs, CISOs, heads of AI and IT administrators. They configure models, skills, integrations, policies and approvals, follow adoption, and review activity.

## Product Purpose

One browser workspace for enterprise AI work: the skills and tools an admin allows, a separate sandbox per chat, approval before risky tool calls, saved files, and a saved history of each turn.

## Brand Personality

Assured, precise, approachable. Say directly what the system is doing; do not be clinical, intimidating or verbose.

## Anti-references

- A generic consumer chatbot: only a transcript and a prompt box.
- Enterprise software that shows its architecture, governance model or configuration before the user needs them.

## Design Principles

1. **Keep the work primary.** Give most of the screen to conversation, drafts and artifacts. Show platform controls only when they apply.
2. **Make consequential actions legible.** At an approval or a policy block, show what will happen, why the user must act, and the choices.
3. **Reveal complexity progressively.** Give everyday users familiar controls and defaults. Show governance and runtime detail to administrators, in context.
4. **Show evidence.** Make status, history, approvals and audit entries specific. Do not use decoration or reassuring text instead of facts.
5. **Use one product language.** Use the same interaction patterns in the workspace, settings and administration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, with screen-reader semantics and browser zoom support. The concrete rules are in `DESIGN.md` (Do's and Don'ts).
